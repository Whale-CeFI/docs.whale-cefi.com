# Legal and Support

This section identifies the organisations behind Whale CeFi, the customer’s legal relationship, asset rights, service eligibility, privacy perimeter, risk disclosures, complaints process, and official contact routes.

Whale CeFi is the product brand. **Whale Foundation**, officially registered
in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, is the current stewardship and
intellectual-property entity. Customer-facing financial services are mapped
to the applicable Whale CeFi operating entity in the Entity and Service
Register.

The separation is intentional: brand stewardship, software ownership, customer contracting, custody, treasury, and regulated obligations are not collapsed into one vague word such as “platform.”
