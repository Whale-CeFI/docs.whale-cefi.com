# Risks, Legal Notes, and Change Log

Progression helps users understand participation; it does not remove product, security, legal, tax, or financial risk.

## Important distinctions

- XP is a progression score, not money, principal, a security, or a guaranteed asset.
- A level, credential, or achievement is not proof of investment competence and does not weaken a mandatory safety control.
- A randomised reward does not guarantee a specific outcome. Published probability describes the configuration across a large number of draws.
- Referral and leaderboard rewards remain subject to valid attribution, eligibility, finalisation, anti-abuse review, and regional rules.
- A temporary boost is not a permanent right. Its funding, scope, duration, cap, stacking rule, and expiry are part of the reward record.
- Product activity performed to obtain XP or another reward carries the same underlying product risk as activity performed for any other reason.

## Regional availability

A progression mechanic can be unavailable or modified by country, age, account type, product status, promotional law, or operational policy. An ineligible mechanic is disabled before participation; a disclaimer does not replace a deterministic block.

## Taxes

An asset bonus, discount, rate boost, collectible, or other benefit can have tax consequences. Whale CeFi records the reward type and value basis where applicable but does not provide personal tax advice.

## Configuration changes

Material changes identify the affected mechanic, old and new version, effective time, reason, treatment of active tasks and existing progress, and appeal route. Confirmed ledger history is not silently rewritten.
