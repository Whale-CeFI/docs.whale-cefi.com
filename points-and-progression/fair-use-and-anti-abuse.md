# Fair Use and Anti-Abuse

Whale CeFi may apply controls such as:

* account and activity validation;
* identity and eligibility checks;
* linked-account and device review;
* referral-abuse detection;
* velocity and suspicious-pace monitoring;
* purchase and reward limits;
* duplicate-event detection;
* funding-pattern analysis;
* assessment-integrity signals;
* temporary holds;
* and manual review.

## Examples of prohibited conduct

* creating or controlling multiple accounts to obtain duplicate progress;
* self-referral or referral rings;
* using false, rented, stolen, or synthetic identity material;
* bots, scripts, macros, or automation not expressly permitted;
* circular deposits or withdrawals designed only to trigger tasks;
* manipulating timing, refunds, or reversals to retain a reward;
* exploiting a bug or configuration mistake;
* buying, selling, transferring, or sharing accounts or credentials;
* misleading other users about rewards or guaranteed outcomes;
* and attempting to discover or bypass confidential anti-abuse controls.

## Possible outcomes

Depending on severity and evidence, Whale CeFi may:

* delay credit;
* request additional verification;
* exclude an event;
* reverse XP or rewards;
* remove leaderboard eligibility;
* suspend a credential;
* restrict the progression system;
* suspend or terminate an account under the applicable terms;
* or refer the matter for legal or compliance review.

## Proportionality

An anomaly is not automatically proof of abuse. Material adverse action should be based on documented rules, attributable evidence, and an appropriate review path. Confidential detection logic may remain undisclosed, but the user should receive a meaningful reason category and access to appeal where required.
