# Achievements

Achievements are permanent or long-lived recognition milestones associated with specific eligible behaviour or learning. Unlike a level, which summarises total XP, an achievement identifies what the user actually accomplished.

Achievement categories may include:

* first actions and onboarding milestones;
* eligible deposit or plan milestones;
* portfolio and product-use patterns;
* responsible behaviour and account longevity;
* progression milestones;
* referral development;
* learning and verified competency;
* seasonal or leaderboard performance;
* chest or collection milestones;
* and special platform events.

## Achievement card

Every card should include:

* name and description;
* rarity;
* locked, tracking, pending, or unlocked state;
* visible progress where appropriate;
* completion date;
* whether the achievement is permanent, seasonal, or revocable;
* reward, if any;
* and the percentage of eligible users who have unlocked it where that statistic is reliable.

## Rarity

Rarity should be calculated from an explicit eligible population and time window. A statement such as “unlocked by 0.3% of users” is misleading unless the denominator, exclusions, and update time are defined.

## Hidden achievements

A hidden achievement may conceal its exact condition until unlocked, but it must not conceal a material financial obligation, paid requirement, legal restriction, or condition that would be unfair to omit.

## Historical achievement library

The old documentation proposed Bronze, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic rarities and examples across deposits, referrals, earnings, longevity, behaviour, chests, and whale-scale actions. The new catalogue should preserve useful creative concepts only after each trigger is supported by the current admin system and reviewed for product, legal, financial, and anti-abuse consequences.
