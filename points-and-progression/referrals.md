# Referrals

## Referral lifecycle

1. A user shares their unique referral link or code.
2. A new user registers through the valid attribution path.
3. The new account completes the published eligibility requirements.
4. Eligible referral activity is validated.
5. The referring user receives the configured reward or progression credit.

## Active referral

The previous public documentation defined an active referral as a referred user who completed identity verification and a minimum qualifying deposit. The new guide must load the current definition from the approved referral configuration and legal terms.

## Referral XP

The old Product Documentation stated that a referrer receives 5% of the XP an active referral earns through quests, generated by the system without reducing the referral’s own XP.

This rule may be republished only after confirming:

* the current percentage;
* which XP sources qualify;
* activation conditions;
* duration;
* caps;
* reversals;
* region and promotion-law eligibility;
* attribution window;
* and treatment of deleted, suspended, refunded, duplicated, or abusive accounts.

## Required dashboard fields

* referral link or code;
* registered referrals;
* active referrals;
* pending or under-review referrals;
* confirmed rewards;
* reversed rewards;
* programme rules and effective version;
* and support access.

## Prohibited conduct

Self-referral, multi-accounting, identity rental, automated sign-ups, circular funding, misleading promotion, unauthorised paid acquisition, and attempts to bypass geographic or eligibility restrictions may result in exclusion, review, suspension, or reversal under the applicable rules.
