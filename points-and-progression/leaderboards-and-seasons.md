# Leaderboards and Seasons

Possible categories include:

* total confirmed XP;
* seasonal XP;
* level or cohort standing;
* eligible asset or product categories;
* verified learning progress;
* or another metric announced for a specific event.

## Leaderboard entry

The page should show:

* category;
* scoring metric;
* season or measurement window;
* update time;
* eligibility;
* tie-breaking rule;
* reward positions;
* moderation status where relevant;
* and finalisation date.

## Provisional and final results

Live rankings should be labelled provisional. A season becomes final only after the published validation period. Accounts under review may be temporarily hidden, marked, or excluded according to the rules.

## Seasons

Every season should publish:

* name;
* start and end time with timezone;
* eligible population;
* scoring method;
* rewards;
* review window;
* finalisation date;
* and rules for cancellations, outages, or material configuration errors.

## Historical reward claims

The old documentation referred to specific Top 100, Top 10, Top 3, and first-place XP or chest rewards. Those values must not be copied into the new guide unless the active season configuration confirms them.
