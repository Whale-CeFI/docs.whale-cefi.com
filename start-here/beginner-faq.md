# Beginner FAQ

### Do I need my own wallet?

The required route depends on the product surface and supported deposit method. When an external wallet is used, it remains responsible for signing the transfer. Whale CeFi never needs the wallet’s seed phrase.

### Is a wallet the same as an account?

No. A Whale CeFi account identifies the user, permissions, product positions and internal financial record. A wallet controls signing authority for blockchain instructions.

### Why can the same asset appear on several networks?

A token can exist on more than one blockchain. The symbol may be identical while the settlement route and contract are different. Always match the exact supported network.

### What is a position?

A position is the accepted record of an amount placed under a specific product version. It binds the asset, term, rate, formula, source policy, fees, risks, maturity and exit rule.

### Am I investing in the Whale CeFi company when I stake?

No. You are a customer using a product. You do not become a shareholder or venture investor merely by opening an earning or staking position.

Project investors finance the company and its growth through separate arrangements. Customer principal remains separate from that corporate capital.

### Where can a high reward rate come from?

Whale CeFi’s early-growth rate has two separately controlled components:

**Monthly Reward Rate = Base Product Reward + Growth Uplift**

The **Base Product Reward** is supported by recognized net economics from approved staking, lending, liquidity, market-neutral or other authorized routes.

The **Growth Uplift** is a temporary addition released from the Marketing Incentive Pool. That pool is funded from the strategic growth and marketing budget provided by project investors.

The uplift is not described as blockchain or protocol yield. It is a separate growth incentive.

Customer principal never becomes the source of another customer’s reward.

### Why use marketing money this way?

Traditional marketing can spend heavily on impressions, influencers, sign-ups and one-time bonuses without creating active long-term users.

Whale CeFi directs part of the early acquisition budget into product value for people who actually enter an eligible position. The goal is to build real participation, early TVL, liquidity, understanding and retention rather than pay only for traffic.

It is marketing through user economics: more of the growth budget reaches active users.

### Why might rates fall as Whale CeFi grows?

The Marketing Incentive Pool is finite. At the beginning, it is distributed across a smaller amount of eligible TVL, so the uplift per unit of capital can be higher.

As eligible TVL grows, the same pool must cover a larger capital base. The Growth Uplift available to **new** positions can therefore decline unless new approved budget or stronger product economics offsets that growth.

TVL is only one input. Liquidity, strategy capacity, reserves, maturity profile, risk limits and operating costs also matter. Whale CeFi does not promise that one user count or TVL number will always produce one specific future rate.

### Can a lower new rate change my existing Locked position?

No. An already accepted Locked position keeps its accepted asset, term, monthly rate and calculation rules until maturity.

A later rate applies to a later product version. Flexible rates can change only prospectively under their notice and effective-time rules.

### What happens when the early uplift becomes smaller?

The long-term ecosystem is designed around more than the rate.

WENI helps users understand data and decisions. XP, seasons and achievements make progress visible. Learn-to-Earn develops user knowledge. These systems are separate from the financial reward ledger, but they give users reasons to remain active after the early Growth Uplift normalizes.

### Is the displayed percentage APR or APY?

Only when it is explicitly labelled that way. Current product percentages are monthly reward rates. An annual comparison is not the same thing as changing the native product unit.

### Do rewards compound automatically?

No. Reward does not silently become principal. Auto-Reinvest is a separate user instruction and creates a new capacity-consuming action.

### Can I exit a fixed-term position early?

Fixed positions remain committed until maturity unless the accepted product version defines a specific exit path.

### Can WENI move my assets?

No. WENI can explain, calculate, simulate and prepare an unsigned proposal. A separate user wallet or approved institutional quorum must authorize the exact payload.

### What proves my balance?

The user statement is derived from append-only double-entry journals. Custody and blockchain evidence corroborate controlled assets, while reconciliation checks agreement between financial and external states.
