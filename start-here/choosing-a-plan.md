# Choosing a Plan

A higher percentage is only one part of the decision. The useful question is: **what exactly am I accepting in exchange for this reward?**

## Flexible and fixed-term plans

|                | Flexible                                       | Fixed term                                   |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Access         | No fixed maturity date                         | 30, 90, 180, or 365 days                     |
| Rate treatment | May change prospectively under the notice rule | Bound to the accepted position version       |
| Reward posting | Daily accrual                                  | Daily accrual                                |
| Exit           | Under the flexible closure rule                | At maturity, except a defined emergency path |
| Best suited to | Assets you may need sooner                     | Assets you can commit for the entire term    |

## Compare plans in the same language

Before choosing, compare:

* the exact asset and network;
* whether the percentage is monthly, annual, or another unit;
* the calculation method and whether compounding exists;
* the projected reward for the actual dates;
* the reward-source mix;
* the lock, maturity, and early-closure rules;
* fees, adjustments, and network costs;
* material risks and the conditions that can delay settlement.

## Monthly rate does not mean APY

Whale CeFi labels the native product unit. A monthly reward rate is not silently renamed APR or APY. If an annualized comparison is shown, it is marked as a comparison and does not change the contractual calculation.

## The liquidity test

Ask one question before selecting a term:

> What is the earliest realistic date I may need this asset?

Choose a term that still works if your plans change. A longer commitment can provide a different reward, but it also removes access for longer.

## The source test

Open the reward-source disclosure. It separates realized external economics from pre-funded incentives and coverage capital. The percentage and its source should make sense together.

## Final review

The final screen binds the amount, asset, network, product version, rate unit, rate value, calculation method, term, fees, risks, source policy, maturity, and exit rule. Any material change expires the review and requires a new confirmation.
