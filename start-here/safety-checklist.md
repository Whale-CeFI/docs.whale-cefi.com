# Safety Checklist

## Protect access

* Use a unique password stored in a trusted password manager.
* Enable every available account-security control.
* Protect the email account and device connected to Whale CeFi.
* Store wallet recovery material offline and away from screenshots or cloud notes.
* Review active sessions and recovery methods after any device change.

## Verify the route

* Enter Whale CeFi through the official saved address or app.
* Select the asset and network separately.
* Confirm the token contract when the route displays it.
* Include the required memo or tag.
* Use a test transfer for an unfamiliar route or destination.

## Verify the product

* Read the rate unit, not only the number.
* Confirm the term and earliest exit date.
* Read the source attribution and material risks.
* Check whether compounding exists or requires a separate instruction.
* Save the final confirmation record.

## Verify support communication

Treat any unsolicited message as untrusted. A legitimate support process does not require your seed phrase, private key, one-time code, or remote control of your device. Urgency, guaranteed recovery, and requests to move funds to a “safe” address are common attack patterns.

## If something looks wrong

1. Stop creating new transfers or approvals.
2. Record the exact screen, time, transaction hash, and account state.
3. Revoke suspicious wallet permissions when safe to do so.
4. Secure the connected email and device.
5. Open a case through the official authenticated support path.
6. Follow the incident state rather than instructions received in private messages.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Never pay an unknown party to “unlock,” “verify,” or “recover” a Whale CeFi balance.
{% endhint %}
