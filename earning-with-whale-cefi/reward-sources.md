# Reward Sources

Whale CeFi does not describe every displayed reward percentage as “staking yield”. A user-facing reward rate can be supported by more than one economic layer, and each layer is accounted for separately.

The important distinction is between **productive external yield** and **pre-funded growth support**.

## 1. Productive external yield

Eligible customer assets may be deployed only through approved routes. Depending on the asset, product version, jurisdiction, liquidity profile and current risk limits, those routes may include:

* native proof-of-stake validation and delegated staking;
* institutional or overcollateralized lending;
* lending to approved centralized counterparties where permitted;
* decentralized lending markets;
* liquidity provision to approved protocols and pools;
* protocol fee capture and time-bounded incentive programmes;
* market-neutral basis, funding or matched-spread strategies;
* contractual rebates, revenue-share arrangements or other specifically approved institutional economics.

These activities can produce economic income, but the amount is variable and each source has its own risks, lockups, counterparty limits, execution costs and recognition rules.

## 2. Pre-funded growth support

Whale CeFi may also support an early product cohort with a finite pool of company capital that has already been committed to the product before the corresponding reward obligation is opened.

This capital can originate from retained company resources or from financing previously provided to the company by its shareholders or strategic investors. The existence of an investment round does **not** mean that the entire investment amount is available for user rewards. Only a formally approved, segregated and capacity-reserved allocation can enter the reward-funding system.

The commercial purpose of this allocation is product bootstrapping: instead of using all acquisition capital only for broad advertising, a defined portion can be directed toward measurable product participation, early user acquisition, liquidity formation, TVL growth, retention and ecosystem expansion.

In that structure, the early user reward can be understood as:

**recognized external yield + approved growth-support allocation = available reward funding, subject to costs, reserves, capacity and risk limits**

This is why an early displayed reward rate may be higher than the underlying native staking yield of the asset itself. The difference is not presented as protocol yield; it is disclosed as a finite, pre-funded incentive component.

## Users are not project investors

A customer who opens an earning or staking position does not become a shareholder, venture investor or equity holder in Whale CeFi merely by using the product. Customer principal remains a customer asset or contractual principal obligation under the applicable product structure. It is not company investment capital and is not recognized as corporate revenue.

New customer principal cannot be used to settle an older customer's reward.

## Why rates can normalize as the platform grows

Growth-support capital is finite. As the active user base and TVL increase, the same approved incentive pool is distributed across a larger capital base, while strategy capacity, liquidity, counterparty limits, reserve requirements and realized external yield must still remain within policy.

TVL is therefore an important rate-setting input, but it is not the only one. New-position rates are reviewed against:

* remaining incentive runway;
* realized and expected external yield;
* active TVL and cohort size;
* strategy and counterparty capacity;
* liquidity and maturity profile;
* custody and network limits;
* reserve and stress requirements;
* operating and execution costs;
* legal and regional availability.

As the platform matures, it is normal for incentive-supported rates offered to **new** positions to move toward a lower, more sustainable level. Whale CeFi does not publish a guaranteed future rate path or promise that a specific percentage will apply at a specific user count or date.

Already accepted Locked positions retain the rate version agreed at opening. Flexible rates may change only prospectively under the applicable notice and effective-time rules.

## Source-attribution schedule

Every plan version binds a funding schedule containing source identifier, legal counterparty or protocol, asset and network, gross-income recognition rule, cash or token settlement, fee and cost waterfall, incentive allocation, budget cap, effective dates and reconciliation account.

Growth-support capital is posted separately from protocol or strategy income. Unrealised token value, projected strategy profit, future fundraising and uncommitted corporate capital are not treated as distributable reward funding.

## Sustainability view

The financial dashboard separates realized external income, committed growth-support capital, reward liability, paid reward, liquidity reserve, remaining incentive runway and uncovered shortfall. A plan stops accepting new positions when its funding, capacity, liquidity or risk rule cannot be met.
