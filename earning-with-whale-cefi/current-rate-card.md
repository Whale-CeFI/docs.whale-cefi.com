### Why a product rate can exceed native staking yield

The displayed percentage is a **product reward rate**, not a claim that the underlying blockchain produces that exact return.

Under Whale CeFi’s early-growth model:

**Monthly Reward Rate = Base Product Reward + Growth Uplift**

The Base Product Reward is supported by recognized net economics from approved strategy routes. The temporary Growth Uplift is released from the Marketing Incentive Pool funded from the strategic growth and marketing budget provided by project investors.

The two sources remain separately attributable. Customer principal is not reward income.

### Why future rates can be lower

The Marketing Incentive Pool is finite. As eligible TVL grows, the Growth Uplift available per unit of new capital can decline unless new approved budget or stronger realized economics offsets that growth.

TVL is only one rate-governance input. Liquidity, strategy capacity, reserves, maturity profile, counterparty exposure, operating cost and risk limits also matter. No future rate is guaranteed by a particular TVL threshold, user count or date.

### Product-version protection

* A Locked position retains the rate version accepted at opening.
* A later public rate applies to later product versions, not retroactively to an existing Locked position.
* Flexible rates may change only prospectively under the applicable notice and effective-time rules.
* Posted reward is not recalculated because a later public rate is lower.

### Minimums, fees and capacity

* Minimum deposit: the equivalent of **USD 50** unless a stricter asset-route record applies.
* Whale CeFi platform deposit fee: **0**, while the active product terms state so.
* Whale CeFi platform withdrawal fee: **0**, while the active product terms state so.
* Network, validator, issuer or third-party charges remain separate.
* A plan stops accepting new exposure when approved funding, liquidity or capacity limits are reached.
