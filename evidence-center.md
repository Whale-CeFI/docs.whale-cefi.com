# Evidence Center

The Evidence Center connects each material Whale CeFi claim to a record that can be inspected, versioned, and independently checked.

![The open ledger](./assets/visuals/heroes/v3/06-open-ledger.png)

A diagram explains how a control is designed. Evidence shows what release, balance, deployment, audit, proof epoch, incident, or decision actually exists.

## Evidence routes

| Question | Record |
|---|---|
| What company and service am I dealing with? | [Entity and Service Register](legal-and-support/entity-and-service-register.md) |
| What rates and terms apply? | [Product Version Register](evidence-center/product-version-register.md) |
| What backs principal and accrued rewards? | [Reserves and Liabilities](evidence-center/reserves-and-liabilities.md) |
| Where does reward funding come from? | [Funding and Capacity](evidence-center/funding-and-capacity.md) |
| What code was audited and remediated? | [Audit Center](evidence-center/audit-center.md) |
| What contracts are deployed? | [Deployment Registry](evidence-center/deployment-registry.md) |
| What assets and networks are supported? | [Asset Registry](evidence-center/asset-registry.md) |
| Is a service operational? | [Service Status and Incidents](evidence-center/service-status-and-incidents.md) |
| What WENI release and evaluations apply? | [WENI System Card](evidence-center/weni-system-card.md) |
| What exact package passed release review? | [Release Manifest](evidence-center/release-manifest.md) |

## Evidence rule

Every record identifies scope, observation time, source, owner, status, freshness rule, limitations, and previous version. A screenshot, logo, marketing statement, wallet list, audit brand, or diagram is not sufficient evidence by itself.
