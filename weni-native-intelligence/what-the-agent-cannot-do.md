# What the Agent Cannot Do

Whale CeFi AI Agent does not become an invisible authority over user assets.

It does not:

* hold or request a private key or seed phrase;
* sign a blockchain transaction on behalf of a user;
* independently move assets;
* guarantee a profit, yield, price, protocol, contract, or transaction outcome;
* eliminate market, liquidity, custody, smart-contract, network, regulatory, operational, or user risk;
* make a stale answer current by expressing it confidently;
* convert a social claim into canonical blockchain or financial state;
* expose raw private chain-of-thought as a substitute for a reviewable decision rationale;
* silently weaken safety controls because a user is experienced or pays for a higher tier;
* or treat the user’s final signature as proof that the preceding analysis was correct.

The system also cannot see what it has not been authorised or technically able to observe. Unsupported contracts, private order flow, incomplete cross-chain state, unavailable providers, off-platform arrangements, legal facts outside the approved registry, and adversarial or manipulated sources may limit the result.

When a material limitation exists, the interface shows it in plain language. The correct system behaviour may be to ask for information, narrow the request, present only an educational explanation, abstain, or block action preparation.

> **Core limitation**\
> Intelligence can improve a decision process. It cannot make an uncertain and risky environment risk-free.
