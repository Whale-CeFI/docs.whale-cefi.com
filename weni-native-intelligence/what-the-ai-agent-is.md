# What the AI Agent Is

The Agent combines four roles without confusing their authority:

## Mentor

It adapts the depth and vocabulary of an explanation while preserving the same material facts, risks, and limitations.

## Research partner

It organises evidence across supported blockchain, protocol, market, product, document, and user-authorised sources.

## Decision-support system

It compares possible paths, declares assumptions, identifies missing information, and presents a rationale that can be reviewed.

## Action-preparation layer

Where the product, technical, and legal perimeter permits, it may prepare an unsigned and expiring request for the user or an authorised enterprise approval system to inspect.

These roles do not make the Agent the source of truth. Authoritative state remains in the appropriate chain, contract registry, product registry, financial ledger, custody evidence, policy system, or user-approved record.

The Agent also does not replace the user’s judgment. It improves the quality and usability of the evidence before a decision without converting uncertainty into a guaranteed outcome.

> **Plain-language definition**\
> Whale CeFi AI Agent is built to help a person understand the evidence, inspect a possible path, and remain in control before anything touches their assets.
