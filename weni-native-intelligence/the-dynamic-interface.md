# The Dynamic Interface

WENI is not limited to a chat window. The interface assembles the right combination of explanation, evidence, calculation, simulation, risk, and action controls for the current task.

## Possible interface components

* concise answer and expandable explanation;
* evidence cards with source and freshness;
* conflict warning;
* contract identity and permission map;
* scenario or simulation card;
* decomposed risk map;
* known-unknowns panel;
* comparison table;
* policy and eligibility result;
* prepared-action review card;
* decision rationale and audit record.

## Progressive disclosure

A first-time user may initially see the conclusion, main reason, material risks, and next safe question. An advanced user may expand block, contract, method, assumption, and tool detail. Both views derive from the same state.

## Voice, screenshot, and document input

Multimodal input is a controlled interaction layer, not permission to treat untrusted content as authoritative. A screenshot may be incomplete or manipulated. A document may be outdated or malicious. Voice may be transcribed incorrectly. Every input requires provenance, parsing, scope, and confirmation appropriate to its impact.

## Interface invariant

The visual system never makes an uncertain or blocked state look complete. Loading, stale, conflicted, unsupported, simulated, prepared, approved, signed, submitted, and confirmed are different product states and are visually distinct.
