# Access Tiers and Enterprise Use

## Tier 0 — Free

The Free tier provides educational explanation, rate-limited intelligence, delayed or reduced data coverage, and a limited set of supported workflows.

## Tier 1 — Holder or Staker

Eligible holders and stakers receive broader real-time data access, higher usage limits, deeper analysis, more advanced Learn-to-Earn paths, or product-integrated workflows where approved.

## Genesis or early-participant benefits

Every multiplier, compute credit, credential, and early-participant benefit is represented by a versioned entitlement record with exact eligibility, issuance, transfer, expiry, abuse, revocation, and legal rules.

## Enterprise

The Enterprise tier provides:

* dedicated or isolated workspaces;
* organisation-controlled data and retention policy;
* approved API and event integration;
* policy-bound research and monitoring;
* higher capacity and defined service levels;
* attested confidential execution for eligible data classes;
* evidence packages, decision records, and audit export;
* role-based review, multisignature, and custody workflow integration.

## Risk Command Center

An enterprise workflow ingests authorised research, monitors defined events, prepares a response proposal, and delivers it to human approvers. The value is faster evidence organisation and response preparation. Final approval and asset movement remain under enterprise policy and signing authority.

Every tier and enterprise capability is activated through a signed entitlement and capability record that binds contract rights, data licensing, legal scope, region, and product configuration.
