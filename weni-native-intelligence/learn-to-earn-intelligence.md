# Learn-to-Earn Intelligence

## Learning path

The Agent may explain a concept, present examples, adapt the next lesson, and identify which prerequisite a user has not understood.

## Assessment

An assessment defines the competency being tested, allowed materials, scoring method, pass threshold, retry rules, expiry, and the evidence needed to treat the result as valid.

## Integrity

Anti-abuse design may combine question variation, reasoning checks, timing and behaviour signals, plagiarism or synthetic-content indicators, challenge prompts, manual review, rate limits, and appeal procedures. No single external detector is treated as infallible proof of cheating.

## Credential

A credential or certification states its issuer, scope, version, issue date, expiry or renewal rule, evidence basis, and whether it is transferable. A credential proves only the tested competency under the stated method.

## Product effect

Competency may influence which explanation depth or learning path is offered. It may support access to advanced educational modules where approved. It never removes mandatory warnings, bypasses suitability or legal policy, or grants the Agent authority over assets.

## Reward effect

XP or other rewards connected to learning remain governed by the Gamification Guide, live progression registry, eligibility rules, caps, anti-abuse system, and applicable terms. Learning recognition and financial-product eligibility are separate decisions.
