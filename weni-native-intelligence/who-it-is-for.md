# Who It Is For

The same market creates different problems for different people. WENI operates on one evidence base with different levels of explanation and workflow support.

## Someone entering crypto for the first time

The Agent explains unfamiliar concepts in sequence, translates technical language, shows why a step matters, and prevents a user from being rushed past an unresolved risk.

The objective is not to hide complexity until the user becomes dependent on the product. It is to reveal the right amount of complexity at the right moment and help the user build real understanding.

## An active crypto participant

The Agent reduces repetitive research, compares supported options, surfaces state changes, explains trade-offs, and preserves a reviewable record of assumptions and evidence.

## An advanced user or analyst

The system exposes deeper provenance, contract identity, block and market context, simulation assumptions, conflict state, freshness clocks, and machine-readable evidence where permitted.

## An institutional team

The Enterprise tier includes controlled workspaces, policy-bound analysis, defined data rights, decision records, approval roles, dedicated capacity, and integration with authorised enterprise workflows.

## One truth, different projections

The interface may simplify language, reorganise detail, or expose additional technical depth. It never creates different underlying facts for different users. A beginner view and a professional view are two projections of the same versioned evidence and policy state.

Safety-critical information cannot disappear because a user selects a simpler interface. Equally, a high tier or advanced credential cannot remove mandatory controls.
