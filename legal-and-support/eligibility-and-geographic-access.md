# Eligibility and Geographic Access

The website, onboarding, application, API, documentation, and Client Agreement read from the same versioned eligibility matrix. If the matrix denies a capability, the interface does not merely display a disclaimer; it blocks the operation.

## Eligibility states

Available, limited, professional-only, institutional-only, temporarily suspended, unsupported, and legally prohibited are distinct. A service can be available in one country through one entity and unavailable elsewhere.

WENI can explain the published rule and gather information. It cannot make the final eligibility decision or override a deterministic deny result.
