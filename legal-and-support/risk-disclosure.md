# Risk Disclosure

You can lose some or all of your principal and any expected reward. Whale CeFi earning products are not bank deposits and are not covered by deposit insurance unless an exact verified policy explicitly states otherwise.

## Material risk classes

* platform insolvency and creditor recovery;
* counterparty, custodian, borrower, or strategy default;
* stablecoin depeg, issuer freeze, or redemption restriction;
* concentration, correlation, leverage, liquidation, and slashing;
* rehypothecation and encumbrance;
* liquidity queues, delayed exits, and suspended routes;
* smart-contract exploit, admin compromise, and oracle failure;
* blockchain congestion, reorganisation, bridge, validator, and finality risk;
* account takeover, fraud, operational error, and reconciliation failure;
* legal, sanctions, tax, and geographic-access change;
* conflicts of interest and related-party exposure;
* WENI error, stale evidence, or misunderstood output.

Each risk record names the owner, indicator, limit, trigger, control, residual risk, customer impact, legal loss allocation, recovery priority, review date, and jurisdiction-specific wording.

Controls reduce probability or impact. They do not make loss impossible.
