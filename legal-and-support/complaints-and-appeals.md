# Complaints and Appeals

A complaint is different from general support, a security incident, a fraud report, an account-restriction appeal, or a privacy request. Each route has a distinct owner and response clock.

## Complaint record

The user receives a case ID, acknowledgement, issue summary, requested remedy, evidence channel, accountable entity, target response time, escalation status, final response, and external escalation route where applicable.

The complaint reviewer can correct service decisions within authority but cannot rewrite blockchain history, invent reserve evidence, suppress an incident, or mark an audit finding closed without the required proof.

## Appeals

Progression, eligibility, fraud, and account restrictions use their own review rules. A final outcome explains the applied rule and effective remedy to the extent permitted by security, privacy, and law.
