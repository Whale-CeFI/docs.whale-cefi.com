# Entity and Service Register

Whale CeFi uses an explicit service map so the user can identify which legal person is responsible for each capability.

## Current group perimeter

| Entity | Location | Role | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whale Foundation — officially registered | Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates | Brand and IP stewardship, governance constitution, research and ecosystem oversight | Does not silently replace the customer’s contracting entity |
| Whale CeFi Services Ltd | Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates | Customer agreement, product operation, account and service responsibility | Does not treat customer principal as company revenue |
| Whale CeFi Custody SPV Ltd | Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates | Segregated custody and settlement perimeter under the approved custody model | Does not originate discretionary product terms |

The authoritative corporate record holds the exact legal form, registration
number, registered address, authority information, permission scope, official
register reference, service mapping, governing law, complaints route, and
effective date. This public documentation states the approved entity map
without reproducing restricted corporate identifiers.

## Service mapping

The plan review identifies the exact contracting entity before confirmation. A change of contracting entity, custody model, governing law, or insolvency treatment is material and creates a new terms version. It cannot be applied silently to an existing locked position.
