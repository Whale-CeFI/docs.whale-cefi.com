Corporate Registration & Transparency This public documentation provides the exact legal mapping of the Whale CeFi ecosystem. The primary operating and contracting entity is [Pulpo Fintech, S.A. de C.V.](https://cnad.gob.sv/assets-providers/pulpo-fintech-s-a-de-c-v/), officially registered and regulated as a Digital Asset Service Provider (Registro No. PSAD-0023) by the National Commission of Digital Assets (CNAD) of the Republic of El Salvador.

All institutional custody arrangements and asset backing are structurally verifiable through our designated Proof of Reserves (PoR) protocols. We do not employ undisclosed offshore shell companies or unregistered trust structures to hold user principal.

## Service mapping

The plan review identifies the exact contracting entity before confirmation. A change of contracting entity, custody model, governing law, or insolvency treatment is material and creates a new terms version. It cannot be applied silently to an existing locked position.
