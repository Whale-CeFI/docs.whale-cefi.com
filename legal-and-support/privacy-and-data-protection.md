# Privacy and Data Protection

Whale CeFi separates account, verification, financial, blockchain, security, progression, support, analytics, and WENI data by purpose and authority.

The Privacy Notice identifies controller, processing purpose, legal basis, categories, recipients, subprocessors, processing regions, retention, security measures, automated decision involvement, user rights, contact route, and effective version.

## WENI-specific boundary

Private account context is retrieved only for an authorised purpose and minimum scope. Screenshots and voice inputs use an isolated ephemeral processing path unless the user expressly saves an item. Secrets and seed phrases are blocked from memory. Financial authority records are not editable through conversational memory.

## Rights

The data-rights route supports access, correction, deletion where legally available, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, and complaint. Identity verification is proportional to the sensitivity of the request.
