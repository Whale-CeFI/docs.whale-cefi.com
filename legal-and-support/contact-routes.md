# Contact Routes

Use the route that matches the issue. Do not rely on unsolicited direct messages, Telegram administrators, or addresses on a non-canonical domain.

| Situation              | Official route                                             | Expected result                                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Account compromise     | Authenticated emergency support or account-compromise form | Freeze and recovery workflow with urgent case ID      |
| Security vulnerability | support@whale-cefi.com and the disclosure form             | Triage, safe-harbour policy, encrypted evidence route |
| Fraud or scam          | Dedicated fraud report                                     | Evidence intake and case tracking                     |
| Formal complaint       | Complaints form and published postal/email route           | Acknowledgement, final response, escalation           |
| Privacy request        | Privacy contact                                            | Identity-verified rights workflow                     |
| General support        | Help Centre ticket                                         | Searchable guidance and case ID                       |

Whale CeFi never asks for a seed phrase, private key, remote desktop access, or transfer to a “safe wallet.”

The repository includes an RFC 9116-compatible [security.txt](../public/.well-known/security.txt).
