# Account and Access

Account security begins before a deposit. This section explains registration, identity verification, authentication, recovery, device controls, sessions, notifications, and the conditions that can limit account access.

Whale CeFi separates identity from asset authority. A verified account establishes who may request an operation. It does not by itself authorise a wallet signature, custody quorum, compliance override, balance correction, or smart-contract change.
