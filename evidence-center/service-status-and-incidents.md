# Service Status and Incidents

Service status reports the current state of login, verification, deposits by route, withdrawals by route, position opening, reward posting, WENI, API, progression, and support.

## Statuses

Operational, degraded performance, partial outage, major outage, maintenance, and suspended-by-policy are distinct states. Each update includes affected scope, observed impact, start time, mitigation, next update time, and incident ID.

An incident record preserves detection, containment, customer impact, financial reconciliation, recovery, root cause, corrective actions, and evidence. A corrected incident is appended; it is not silently removed.

Status communication cannot replace the in-product operation timeline. A global component may be operational while an individual transaction remains under review.
