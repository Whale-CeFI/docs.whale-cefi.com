# Funding and Capacity

Whale CeFi opens new fixed reward obligations only within approved funding and capacity limits.

## Funding waterfall

1. Realised external income attributable to the approved strategy or counterparty.
2. A segregated prefunded reward pool committed to the product version.
3. Explicitly allocated platform capital within the approved risk limit.

Customer principal is not recognised as reward income and cannot settle an older customer reward.

## Capacity record

The capacity record shows principal liabilities, accrued reward liabilities, recognised reserves, immediately liquid reserves, encumbered assets, prefunded rewards, total and liquid coverage, remaining capacity, observation time, attestation status, and stress limits.

When a threshold is breached, the system blocks new exposure before it changes an existing obligation. Remediation can include capacity reduction, new funding, strategy reduction, exit-only state, or a formal incident process.
