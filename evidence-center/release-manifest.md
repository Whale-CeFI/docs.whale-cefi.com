# Release Manifest

The manifest binds the documentation commit and package hash to the Product Truth snapshot, legal sign-off, finance and solvency review, security review, privacy review, WENI evaluation release, approved regions and capabilities, open exceptions, accountable owners, effective time, and rollback target.

A build-integrity pass proves that files, links, schemas, registries, and hashes are coherent. It does not by itself prove legal authority, solvency, security, or truth. Those gates remain separately named and signed.

The machine-readable source is [release-manifest.json](../data/release-manifest.json).
