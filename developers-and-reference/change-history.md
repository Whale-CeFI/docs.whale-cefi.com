# Change History

A chronological record of product, calculation, contract, policy, audit and documentation changes.

The changelog records every material change with effective date, affected pages and registries, previous version, new version, reason, approver and migration impact.

Change classes include product terms, rate schedule, supported asset or network, calculation method, contract deployment, audit or finding status, custody policy, legal perimeter, AI capability, privacy rule and documentation correction.

Historical URLs remain valid. A superseded page points to its replacement and explains the effective interval.

## Release discipline

Documentation changes are reviewed with the product or system change that makes them necessary. A feature cannot be promoted without its terms, risk disclosure, registry record, help path and change entry. Emergency corrections record the incident or evidence that triggered them.
