# Security Contact and Disclosure

How researchers and users report vulnerabilities, account compromise and suspected fraud.

Security reports use the published security channel and include the affected URL, contract, account surface or component; a reproducible description; impact; timestamps; and safe supporting material.

Do not include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, access tokens, unnecessary personal data or funds. Do not exploit other users, interrupt service or move assets to prove a finding.

The acknowledgement gives a case identifier, severity-triage window, safe communication route and next-update time. Urgent account compromise uses the dedicated authenticated support path.

The disclosure policy defines safe-harbour conditions, prohibited testing, in-scope assets, response targets, severity method, remediation validation, publication coordination and reward rules where applicable. Reports and artifacts are access-controlled and retained under the security evidence policy.
