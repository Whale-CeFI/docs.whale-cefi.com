# WENI — Native Intelligence

![WENI — intelligence with human-controlled authority](./assets/visuals/heroes/06-weni-human-control.png)

Staking was the first layer.

The next layer is intelligence.

Whale CeFi AI Agent is a crypto-native mentor and intelligence system. It understands the person using it — their experience, their goal, the evidence available, and the way they make sense of the market.

For someone entering crypto for the first time, the Agent clears away noise and turns an overwhelming industry into a path they can follow without having to master every term on day one.

For experienced users and professional teams, it reduces the work behind finding, checking, comparing, and organising information across a market that never stops moving.

The Agent does not simply return an answer. It explains what is happening, identifies what remains uncertain, prepares a possible next step, and gives the user enough context to judge it.

The decision remains with the user. Nothing touches a user’s assets until the relevant action has been reviewed and explicitly authorised through the approved signing path.

This is not a generic chatbot with crypto added on top. It is not an autonomous trading bot controlling user funds. It is an intelligence layer built around the person using it — not the other way around.
