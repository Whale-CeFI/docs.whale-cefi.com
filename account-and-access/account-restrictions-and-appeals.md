# Account Restrictions and Appeals

## Scope-first restriction

The narrowest safe restriction is used. A restriction can apply to a login, withdrawal, deposit route, asset, product, API credential, progression reward, geographic capability, or the full account. The interface distinguishes a temporary operational hold from a legal freeze or permanent closure.

## What the notice contains

* restriction category;
* affected capabilities;
* effective time;
* whether balances and open positions remain visible;
* actions the user can still take;
* evidence or information required;
* review eligibility and deadline;
* case ID and next update time.

Confidential detection methods, third-party reports, or legally restricted information may be withheld, but the user is not left with an unexplained balance change.

## Appeal

An appeal is reviewed by a role that did not make the original discretionary decision when independence is required. The outcome records the evidence considered, rules applied, remedy, effective time, and any external complaint route.
