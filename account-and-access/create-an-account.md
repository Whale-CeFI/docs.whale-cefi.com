# Create an Account

An account gives you a controlled identity inside Whale CeFi. It is the place where eligibility, product consent, balances, positions, progression, support cases, and security events are associated with one verified user.

## Before you begin

Use only **whale-cefi.com**. Create a unique password that you do not reuse on an exchange, email account, social network, or another financial service. Secure the email address first because a compromised mailbox can weaken recovery and notification controls.

Registration records the applicable Terms, Privacy Notice, Risk Disclosure, and country-specific service perimeter. The exact versions and acceptance time remain available in the account’s legal history.

## Registration flow

1. Enter the required account information.
2. Confirm control of the email address.
3. Establish the primary authentication method.
4. Complete identity and eligibility checks.
5. Review mandatory security and risk notices.
6. Enter the authenticated dashboard.

The system creates an immutable account identifier. Email addresses, wallet addresses, support handles, and profile names are attributes; none of them replaces the canonical account ID.

## What registration does not do

Registration does not create a deposit, activate a plan, guarantee regional access, approve a withdrawal, or make WENI an authorised agent. Each of those events has its own eligibility and consent state.

## Safe first-session checklist

* Verify the browser address and TLS connection.
* Enable the strongest available second factor.
* Store recovery material outside the device used for daily access.
* Review active sessions and trusted devices.
* Configure security notifications.
* Learn the difference between an account confirmation and a wallet signature.

Whale CeFi support never needs your wallet seed phrase, private key, full authentication secret, or remote control of your device.
